# Naming: poweragents (working name)

**What it is.** A desktop app (macOS, Windows) that gives people who don't program an AI agent set up
the way developers set up their own: it works in their projects, browses, runs on schedules and keeps
a memory of them in plain files. Individuals first; money later from companies (shared team
workspaces). Product design: `docs/design/poweragents/`.
**Space.** Personal AI agents and assistants (Claude Cowork, OpenWork, ChatGPT agents, Lindy, etc.).
**Audience.** Non-technical people, including "AI native" chat users; later their companies.
**Languages.** English name; the app ships in Polish and English, so the name must also be easy for
Polish speakers. *(Inferred by the agent, not yet confirmed.)*
**TLDs.** Checked: .com, .app, .ai, plus `get<name>.com`. A bare `.app` or `get<name>.com` is
acceptable to the user (round 1) — a bare .com is not required.

## What the name should make you feel

In the user's words (round 0): accessible, approachable, easy — and at the same time capability and
strength. "An agent that is very easy to use but gives you very large possibilities and really
changes how I work and how I live."

## Standing preferences

- `<name>.app` and `get<name>.com` are fine (round 1).

- Open to the currently popular direction of a good-sounding human first name; the user's own ideas
  were Jerry and Jer (round 0).

## Rejected directions

- "Power" names in the Power Rangers vein — "Power Agent" and similar are taken (round 0).
